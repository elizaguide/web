2. Onboarding & Network Access

As a qualified Mindvalley member, I want to easily access Finerminds so I can join my community
P1
Sarah has completed her one-year Mindvalley membership and finished 8 programs. She wants seamless access to Finerminds without complicated approval processes.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • System automatically verifies Mindvalley qualification status
  • One-click signup with Mindvalley SSO integration
  • Immediate access to relevant networks based on completed programs
  • Onboarding flow explains three-level architecture
  • Profile setup suggests networks and groups to join
As a non-Mindvalley user, I want to apply for membership through peer approval
P1
David works in corporate but hasn't joined Mindvalley yet. He hears about Finerminds from Sarah and wants to apply through the peer approval system.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • Application form captures bio, business interests, and growth goals
  • System requires 3 member endorsements with 0 rejections
  • Members can endorse with personal recommendations
  • Approval process takes 48-72 hours maximum
  • Clear status updates throughout approval process
As a new member, I want guided onboarding to understand the platform value
P1
Marcus just joined and feels overwhelmed by networks, groups, and discussions. He needs clear guidance on how to get value from the platform.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • Interactive tutorial explaining Networks → Groups → Discussions
  • Suggested networks based on profile interests
  • Recommended first groups to join with member testimonials
  • Example discussions to browse before participating
  • 30-day challenge or goal to drive early engagement

3. Network & Group Discovery

As a member, I want to discover relevant networks that match my interests and goals
P1
Lisa wants to find networks beyond just Mindvalley Membership - she's interested in AI business topics and conscious leadership.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • Browse networks by category (Business, Wellness, Technology, etc.)
  • AI-suggested networks based on profile and program completion
  • Network preview shows member count, recent activity, and key discussions
  • Member testimonials and success stories for each network
  • Clear joining process with qualification requirements
As a network member, I want to find groups that dive deeper into my specific interests
P1
Sarah joined Wellness Founders Society but wants more focused groups like AI Mastery, London Group, and Female Founders.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • Group discovery within networks with filtering options
  • Group activity preview and member expertise highlights
  • One-click joining for public groups
  • Request access flow for private/invitation groups
  • Ability to create new groups if none exist for specific topic
As a group member, I want to see what discussions are happening to join relevant conversations
P1
Marcus joined the London Group but wants to see which discussions are active and which ones match his coaching expertise.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • Discussion list shows activity level, member count, and topic focus
  • Preview of recent messages and shared resources
  • Filter discussions by recency, popularity, and relevance
  • Join discussions directly or request access for private ones
  • Bookmark interesting discussions for later participation

4. Discussion Creation & Management

As a group member, I want to create focused discussions on topics I'm passionate about
P1
Sarah sees lots of interest in AI tools but no dedicated discussion. She wants to create "Clawdbot for Content Creation" to share strategies and build resources.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • One-click discussion creation from group interface
  • Clear title and description fields with character guides
  • Configurable invite limits and privacy settings
  • Immediate member invitation flow with smart suggestions
  • Automatic welcome message and purpose explanation
As a discussion creator, I want full admin control to manage the conversation quality
P1
Lisa created an AI discussion but needs to manage membership, set guidelines, and ensure conversations stay focused and valuable.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • Add/remove members with bulk invitation tools
  • Set and update discussion guidelines/rules
  • Pin important messages and shared resources
  • Moderate discussions with message deletion capabilities
  • Transfer admin rights or add co-moderators
As a discussion participant, I want to contribute meaningfully and access shared resources
P1
Marcus joined Sarah's Clawdbot discussion and wants to share his automation workflows while accessing resources others have contributed.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • Real-time messaging with rich text formatting
  • File and link sharing with preview generation
  • Search through discussion history and resources
  • React to messages and create threaded conversations
  • Invite other relevant group members to join

5. Cross-Platform Experience

As a mobile user, I want seamless chat experience on my phone
P1
David is commuting and wants to participate in discussions, check notifications, and respond to messages from his phone with the same quality as desktop.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • Native mobile app with push notifications
  • Offline message queueing and sync when reconnected
  • Mobile-optimized chat interface with gesture controls
  • Quick actions for common tasks (invite, share, react)
  • Consistent data sync between web and mobile
As a web user, I want powerful multi-tasking capabilities for complex discussions
P2
Lisa works primarily on desktop and wants advanced features like multiple discussion windows, rich document sharing, and comprehensive search.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • Multi-panel interface with discussion list + active chat
  • Advanced file upload with drag-and-drop support
  • Comprehensive search across all discussions and networks
  • Keyboard shortcuts for power users
  • Browser notifications for important messages

6. Community Building & Networking

As a network member, I want to organize and attend meaningful events
P2
Marcus wants to organize a breathwork workshop for London Group members and create lasting connections beyond just online chat.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • Event creation within networks and groups
  • RSVP system with attendee visibility (opt-in)
  • Event-specific discussions for coordination
  • Post-event networking and follow-up discussions
  • Integration with calendar systems
As a member, I want to find collaboration opportunities and business partnerships
P2
Sarah is looking for a technical co-founder for her wellness app, while Lisa needs business development help for her AI startup.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • "I'm Looking For" board at network and group levels
  • Skill-based member matching and recommendations
  • Private introduction facilitation between members
  • Success story tracking and community celebration
  • Business partnership formation support

7. Content Quality & Moderation

As a community member, I want high-quality discussions free from spam and off-topic content
P1
All personas value the exclusive, high-quality nature of Finerminds and want it maintained through effective moderation and community standards.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • Community guidelines clearly communicated
  • Peer reporting system for inappropriate content
  • Quick admin response to moderation issues
  • Automatic spam detection and prevention
  • Quality metrics and community health monitoring
As a network admin, I want tools to maintain community quality and culture
P2
Network administrators need powerful tools to maintain the conscious, growth-oriented culture that makes Finerminds valuable.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • Member approval/removal capabilities
  • Discussion monitoring and intervention tools
  • Community health analytics and reporting
  • Guidelines enforcement with escalation procedures
  • Recognition systems for valuable contributors
