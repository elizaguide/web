TRANSFORMATION ROADMAP

6-Phase Implementation Plan

Complete roadmap for implementing BARI's strategic frameworks across all Mindvalley divisions. From quick wins to full transformation over 12 months.

🚀 Immediate Quick Wins (Week 1-2)

Start with high-impact, low-effort changes that demonstrate the BARI approach immediately. Build momentum before tackling complex systemic changes.

📝 Through-Line Messaging Test

Update next summit's speaker briefings to reinforce "Consciousness Engineering makes you extraordinary." Measure belief reinforcement consistency.

Impact: Immediate messaging alignment • Effort: 2 hours • Owner: Summit Team

🎯 Julie-Focused Ad Test

A/B test one ad creative with Julie-specific outcome messaging: "Get 15 hours back per week" vs current feature-focused ads.

Impact: Conversion rate validation • Effort: 1 day • Owner: Marketing Team

⏰ 30-Day Program Pilot

Convert one existing 5-month mastery to 30-day intensive format. Test compressed time = higher value hypothesis.

Impact: Completion rate improvement • Effort: 1 week • Owner: Academy Team

📊 Baseline Metrics

Establish current baselines: completion rates, refund rates, conversion rates, Julie persona percentage in audience.

Impact: Measurement foundation • Effort: 3 days • Owner: Analytics Team

🚨 Critical Dependencies & Blockers

🔴 High Risk Dependencies

  • Leadership Alignment: Vishen and executive team must champion through-line consistency
  • Content Team Training: All creators must understand and apply BARI frameworks
  • Speaker Management: External speakers need strict briefing adherence
  • Technical Resources: App and platform changes require development capacity

🟡 Medium Risk Dependencies

  • Customer Education: Existing customers need to understand program changes
  • Partner Alignment: Affiliates and partners must adapt messaging
  • Resource Reallocation: Budget shifts for high-ticket program development
  • Market Testing: Julie persona validation in different regions

🟢 Manageable Dependencies

  • Marketing Campaigns: Ad creative updates can be phased gradually
  • Community Management: Culture shift can happen organically with guidance
  • Analytics Setup: New metrics can be implemented incrementally
  • Feedback Loops: Customer response monitoring systems
