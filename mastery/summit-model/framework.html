8 Core Principles (Always Apply)

1

Through-line Above All

Messaging, content, and offer must align around one core belief and one clear outcome.

2

Julie is the Right-Fit Client

40-60 year old solopreneur, overwhelmed, wants more time for family. Sell outcomes, not mechanisms.

3

One Belief, 10 Ways

(Hormozi) Never scatter messaging. Every speaker reinforces same transformation from different angle.

4

Sell Outcome, Not Mechanism

"When it's hot outside, it's cold inside." Air conditioning, not freon. 15 hours back, not "5 AI clones."

5

Simplicity Converts

If you have to explain it, cut it. Fourth grader test. One offer. No feature stacks.

6

Method First

Without a defined method (≤6 steps), nothing else works. The method IS the through-line.

7

Compressed Time = Higher Value

$997/30 days beats $997/6 months. Shorter programs, higher prices, better results.

8

The 3-Day Cadence

Day 1: pure value, no selling. Day 2: main pitch (afternoon). Day 3: scripted Q&A repitch (15 min CTAs).
