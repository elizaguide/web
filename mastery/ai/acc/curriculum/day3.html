Hour 2: BUILD Your Video AI Clone

Hands-on creation of voice clone and visual avatar, with immediate testing and quality assessment.

01:00 - 01:20 | Voice Clone Creation

Phase 1: Voice Training Session

Everyone participates simultaneously - record voice training data with guided prompts for optimal AI learning.

🎙️ Voice Training Protocol
Recording Requirements:
• 10-15 minutes of clear, varied speech
• Multiple emotions and tones
• Different sentence structures and content types
• Consistent recording quality and environment

Provided Prompts:
1. Professional introduction (formal tone)
2. Passionate explanation of favorite topic (animated)
3. Storytelling segment (narrative style)
4. Q&A responses (conversational)
5. Call-to-action delivery (persuasive)

🎯 Quality Control

Monitor recording quality in real-time. Help students with microphone positioning, background noise reduction, and emotional range expansion. The voice training data quality determines clone success.

01:20 - 01:40 | Visual Avatar Development

Phase 2: Avatar Creation & Customization

While voice clones process, students create visual avatars using photo training datasets and customize movement patterns.

📸 Photo Requirements & Strategy
Optimal Training Dataset:
• 5-10 high-quality photos with varied angles
• Consistent lighting and professional quality
• Multiple expressions (neutral, smiling, thoughtful)
• Clear face visibility and appropriate framing

Platform Upload Process:
• Photo selection and cropping optimization
• Quality assessment and platform feedback
• Initial avatar generation and preview
• Customization options: gestures, expressions, movements

🎯 Brand Alignment Check

Critical: Ensure avatars maintain professional authenticity. Help students balance realistic representation with brand consistency. Avoid uncanny valley effects that damage credibility.

01:40 - 02:00 | Testing & Content Creation Challenge

Live Video Clone Testing

Content Creation Challenge: Students create 60-90 second authentic video content using their AI twins.

🎬 Challenge Structure
Step 1: Script Your Signature Message (5 min)
• Choose topic you're passionate about
• Write 60-90 second script in your authentic voice
• Include your typical gestures and expressions

Step 2: Generate AI Video (10 min)
• Input script to AI avatar platform
• Apply your trained voice clone
• Generate and download initial video

Step 3: Quality & Authenticity Assessment (5 min)
• Review video for authenticity vs. efficiency
• Identify areas for improvement
• Plan use cases for deployment
🎤 Volunteer Showcase

2-3 volunteers share their AI videos with the group:
• How authentic does it look and sound?
• What worked well vs. what needs refinement?
• Where would you use this in your content strategy?
• How does it compare to your actual on-camera presence?
