🛡️ BRAND PROTECTION & CRISIS MANAGEMENT

Content Guidelines

  • Never Post About: Personal family details, financial specifics, internal conflicts
  • Always Fact-Check: Statistics, quotes, research claims
  • Cultural Sensitivity: Global audience awareness, inclusive language
  • Legal Compliance: SEC guidelines for public companies, privacy laws

Crisis Response Protocol

  1. Acknowledge Quickly: Respond within 2 hours if controversial
  2. Take Responsibility: Own mistakes, focus on learning
  3. Redirect to Values: Connect back to core mission and values
  4. Follow-Up Content: Turn controversy into teaching moment
