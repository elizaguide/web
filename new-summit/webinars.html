🔄 What to Change

❌ Old 90-Minute Approach

  • Too long, attention drops after 60 minutes
  • Q&A at the end feels purely transactional
  • Teaching too much content overwhelms audience
  • No upfront time commitment promise
  • Questions feel random and unstructured

✅ New 60-Minute Strategy

  • Promise: "Done in exactly one hour + bonus"
  • Simple concept taught deeply but quickly
  • Strategic Q&A that naturally repitches
  • Hook for staying until bonus reveal
  • Scripted questions tied to product benefits
💡 Key Insight: "There's only so much I can do in an hour, so I created this [product]. Logically, this makes sense to your audience."

🧠 Understanding Your Audience Psychology

🧠 Logical Buyers (70% of Mindvalley audience)

  • Mostly women who need emotional language to connect
  • Ask questions as part of their buying process
  • Gather information before making decisions
  • Need Q&A to get out of their heads and into their hearts
  • Require heart-centered stories during repitch

❤️ Emotional Buyers (30% of audience)

  • "Love it, where's my credit card?" mentality
  • Make quick decisions during the main pitch
  • Hunter/gatherer mentality - decisive action
  • Convert immediately without needing Q&A
  • Less questions needed to make purchase decision
🎪 Q&A Strategy: "My team tells me you have questions—and that's normal. Let me address the top ones..." then tie every answer back to program benefits.

📝 Content & Teaching Strategy

🎯 The "Simple but Deep" Approach

Instead of teaching multiple complex concepts, focus on ONE simple but powerful method that creates an "aha moment."

Buyer Psychology Journey:

  1. "I didn't know you could do that" (Pre-webinar hook)
  2. "Even I could do that" (During simple teaching)
  3. "I need to learn this" (When you make the offer)

❌ Common Mistakes

  • Teaching too much in short time
  • Complex, overwhelming methods
  • Making them feel "this is too hard"
  • No clear, single outcome

✅ Winning Strategy

  • One simple concept, taught deeply
  • Fast-paced but achievable
  • "Anyone can do this" feeling
  • Clear, single transformation promised

❓ Strategic Q&A That Converts

The Q&A Framework

Q&A isn't just answering questions—it's strategically repitching your offer through helpful responses.

Example Q&A Repitch:

"A lot of you ask about our coaching sessions and how they work. They typically start at 11 AM and go until 5 PM, where we go deep on the topics that support your 30-day transformation. Remember yesterday when we talked about the 30-day framework? Well, the first 30 days is where you get your foundation, but then you want to go deeper on each topic. The coaching days give us time to do that work together..."

Notice: This answers the question while repitching two key deliverables (coaching + 30-day framework) without feeling salesy.

✅ DO This

  • Pre-script top 5-7 questions
  • Tie every answer to program benefits
  • Use heart-centered stories
  • "My team tells me..." introduction

❌ Don't Do This

  • Pull out pitch deck again
  • Repeat same details from earlier
  • Answer questions without connection to offer
  • Say "Let me go through features again"

🚀 Implementation Checklist

1. Restructure Timing

Cut webinars to 60 minutes with upfront promise. Test the 15-15-15-15 structure.

2. Simplify Content

Focus on ONE simple but powerful concept instead of trying to teach everything.

3. Script Q&A Sessions

Prepare 5-7 strategic questions that let you repitch program benefits naturally.

4. Add Bonus Hook

"Turn off distractions, stay focused—I have a bonus for those who stay until the end."

5. Test Emotional Language

Use "I feel you" and heart-centered language for your predominantly female audience.

6. Track Drop-off Points

Monitor when people leave and adjust content/pacing accordingly.
