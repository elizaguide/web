⏱️ New Event Structure

❌ OLD: 90-Minute Webinar

  • Too long, attention drops
  • Q&A at the end feels pitchy
  • Teaching too much content
  • Same offer repeated

✅ NEW: 60-Minute Webinar

  • Promise: "Done in exactly one hour"
  • Simple concept, deep but fast
  • Strategic Q&A that repitches
  • Hook for staying until bonus
🎯 Webinar Structure: 15 min content/connection → 15 min pain/solution → 15 min decision → 15 min Q&A repitch

3-Day Summit Flow

📚 Day 1

Content, Community, Connection

  • • No selling at all
  • • Build trust and value

  • • Create community

💰 Day 2

Main Sales Pitch

  • • More content + pain building
  • • THE sales presentation
  • • Emotional buyers convert
  • • Afternoon timing optimal

❓ Day 3

Scripted Q&A + Soft Repitch

  • • Address logical buyers
  • • Scripted questions
  • • Repitch through answers
  • • Close logical buyers

🧠 Methodology Creation Framework

❌ Current: Speaker Buffet

"Here are 20 amazing experts talking about different aspects of AI/manifesting/etc."

  • Speakers choose their own topics
  • No connection between sessions
  • People can skip sessions
  • No urgency to attend all

✅ New: Method-Based

"Here's our proven 5-step framework taught by world-class experts."

  • Each speaker teaches ONE step
  • Sequential building blocks
  • "Miss one step = miss the method"
  • Must attend everything
💡 Key Insight: "People buy frameworks, not random collection of tips. Every speaker must teach to YOUR method, not their own."

🎯 Audience Psychology: Logical vs Emotional Buyers

65-70%
Women in audience (logical buyers)
Q&A
Critical for logical buyer conversion

🧠 Logical Buyers (Mostly Women)

  • Need emotional language to connect
  • Ask questions as part of buying process
  • Gather information before deciding
  • Need Q&A to get out of their head
  • Require heart-centered stories

❤️ Emotional Buyers (Mostly Men)

  • "Love it, where's my credit card?"
  • Make quick decisions
  • Hunter/gatherer mentality
  • Convert during main pitch
  • Less questions needed
🎪 Q&A Strategy: "My team tells me you have questions - let me address the top ones" then tie every answer back to program benefits

📝 Marketing & Messaging Changes

❌ Stop Saying

  • "Here's our detailed schedule"
  • "20 amazing speakers on different topics"
  • "Pick the sessions you want"
  • "Can't make Day 2? Join us Day 3!"

✅ Start Saying

  • "Here's our proven framework"
  • "These experts teach our method"
  • "Every session builds on the last"
  • "Miss a little, miss a lot"
🔒 No More Detailed Agendas: Only show speakers, topics, and times. Let them trust your methodology instead of cherry-picking sessions.

2. Restructure Speaker Briefs

3. Rewrite Landing Pages

🎪 The Big Picture Transformation

FROM: "Buffet of amazing experts teaching different things"
TO: "Proven step-by-step system taught by the world's best teachers"

The goal is to move from being seen as a collection of random speakers to being seen as the definitive source for a specific methodology. When someone wants to learn your framework, they should think: "I need to attend this entire event because every piece builds on the last."
