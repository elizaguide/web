Two Strategic Transformations

🎯 Webinar Strategy

Transform 90-minute endurance tests into high-converting 60-minute experiences.

  • 60-minute promise: "Done in exactly one hour"
  • 15-15-15-15 structure: Content → Pain → Decision → Q&A
  • Audience psychology: 70% logical buyers need strategic Q&A
  • Simple but deep: One concept taught powerfully

🎪 Summit Strategy

Move from speaker buffets to method-based 3-day experiences with proven through-lines.

  • 5-step methodology: Each speaker teaches one step
  • Day flow: Day 1 content → Day 2 pitch → Day 3 Q&A
  • "Miss a little, miss a lot": Sequential building blocks
  • No more agendas: Trust methodology over transparency

🚨 What's Not Working

The "Buffet Event" Problem

Webinar Issues:

  • 90-minute attention span problem
  • Teaching too much content
  • Q&A feels purely transactional

Summit Issues:

  • Speaker buffet allows cherry-picking
  • No cohesive methodology
  • Attendance drops Day 1 → Day 3
💡 Root Cause: We're selling random collections of tips instead of proven, step-by-step methodologies. People buy frameworks, not buffets.
