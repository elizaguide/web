Professional Tips & Best Practices

Insider secrets for creating the most realistic and engaging AI avatar content

📸

Character Sheet Strategy

Create a fictional persona loosely based on yourself to avoid perfectionism and enable creative freedom. This reduces overthinking about appearance details.

🎯

Close-Up Shots Win

Generate images from shoulders up for most realistic lip-sync. The closer the shot to the face, the better HeyGen performs with mouth movements.

🗣️

Conversational Scripts

Write scripts that sound like podcast conversations. Add audio tags like [laughs], [sighs], [pause] to create natural delivery and realistic gestures.

⚡

Batch Production

Create 30 days of scripts in advance. Generate multiple voiceovers and save avatar poses to streamline daily content creation to just copy-paste-generate.

🎨

Nano Banana Only

Stick to Nano Banana Pro for all avatar images. It's the most realistic AI image model available and provides consistent, professional results.

🎭

Motion Control

Use "Calm and Composed" preset in HeyGen's advanced settings. Avoid "Excited and Emotive" which can look unnatural for professional content.

Script Templates & Prompts

Ready-to-use prompts and script structures for natural-sounding content

ChatGPT Podcast Script Generator

Write a 15 to 22-second spoken script designed specifically for text-to-speech inside 11 Labs. The script should sound conversational, like someone speaking naturally in a podcast interview. Include natural speech patterns like "um," "you know," "right," and audio tags in brackets like [pause], [laughs], [sighs] to create realistic delivery. The topic is: [YOUR TOPIC]. Make it engaging and authentic, as if the person just started speaking mid-conversation.

Sample Avatar Podcast Prompts

Professional woman in modern podcast studio, wearing elegant business attire, sitting at professional microphone setup with warm lighting, contemporary studio background, speaking directly to camera, confident and engaging expression, high-end podcast equipment visible, clean modern aesthetic
Female entrepreneur in stylish podcast studio, casual professional clothing, warm studio lighting, sitting at podcast desk with microphone, clean modern background, speaking passionately about business, authentic and approachable demeanor, high-quality studio setup
