The 80% Rule That Changes Everything

"80% of any AI problem is just gathering data" - The Economist, 2020

Why Data Collection Matters

In 2020, Vishen read a single line in The Economist that changed how he approached AI: "80% of any AI problem is just gathering data."

This insight led to:

  • Millions made investing in AI companies (including NVIDIA)
  • Building Mindvalley Supplements website with zero designers
  • Transforming health outcomes through data-driven insights
  • Revolutionary meeting intelligence and team analysis

Case Study: How Data Saved Vishen's Health

The Problem

March 17 - May 25: Waistline grew from 80cm to 87cm in just 7 weeks

🇪🇺 Europe (Normal)

Clean food with strict regulations

🇲🇾 Malaysia + 🇺🇸 America

7 weeks of less regulated food = weight gain

"Something's wrong with what I'm eating. I'm going to photograph everything and feed it into ChatGPT."
1

The Simple Prompt

"I need you to be my eating advisor. I'm going to tell you what I eat every day and you're going to help me with my macros."

No genius required - just grumpy human with tight pants

2

The Data Collection

Photographed every meal, recorded waistline measurements, fed everything to ChatGPT

ChatGPT can calculate macros and calories from photos

3

The Breakthrough Discovery

AI Insight: "You're likely gaining belly fat due to excessive dietary fat plus not enough fiber."

97% of men don't eat enough fiber. Fiber feeds your gut microbiome, not just you.

4

The Result

6 weeks: 86.8cm → 80.65cm waistline

All because he became a "data squirrel"

Meeting Intelligence: Your AI Executive Assistant

Be a Mad Squirrel 🐿️

Deliberately gather and store meeting data. Everything documented, everything stored, everything analyzed.

📝 Google Gemini Meeting Notes

Click "Record" in Google Calendar meetings → automatic transcripts emailed to you

🎙️ iPhone/Android Recording

Open memo app, hit record for offline meetings → upload transcript to AI

💻 ChatGPT Desktop App

New record button for up to 2-3 hours of meeting capture

🥣 Granola (Tomorrow's Tool)

Automatic meeting recording and organization based on calendar

What AI Can Build From Your Meetings

📋 Beautiful Meeting Summaries

Upload meeting notes to Claude and ask: "Build a simple webpage that summarizes this meeting and shows next steps with responsibilities."

Pro Tip: Ask for webpages, not just text summaries. You can share webpages with your team!

🧠 Team Psychology Analysis

Upload 4+ meetings and ask: "Analyze these meetings. How is my team evolving? What underlying currents do you find?"

AI can identify team dynamics, leadership patterns, and individual contributions you might miss.

📊 Leadership Intelligence

Get insights on team member contributions, tensions, and growth patterns across multiple meetings.

Example: "Bruno is the realist in a visionary room" - valuable leadership insights

🎨 Custom Styled Pages

Tell Claude: "Make this blue and red" or "Put a picture of a banana and make it disgustingly colorful"

AI follows your design preferences, no matter how specific or unusual
